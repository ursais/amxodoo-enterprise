This module registers the Volkswagen CFDI addenda for Mexican electronic
invoicing on Odoo Enterprise and adds invoice fields used by the addenda
XML.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

1. Go to **Accounting > Customers > Customers**.
2. Open the Volkswagen partner.
3. Add **Addenda Volkswagen** to the partner addendas.

Usage
=====

1. Create an invoice for a partner with **Addenda Volkswagen**.
2. Fill the VW fields on the **Addenda Volkswagen** tab and line
   material refs.
3. Confirm and stamp the invoice.
