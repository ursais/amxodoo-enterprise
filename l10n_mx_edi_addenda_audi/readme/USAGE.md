To use this module, you need to:

1. Go to Accounting > Customers > Invoices
2. Create a new invoice for Audi
3. In the "Other Information" tab, fill in the Customer reference field
4. In the "Addenda Audi" tab, fill in the business unit and applicant email
   fields
