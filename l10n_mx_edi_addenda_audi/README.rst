This module registers the Audi CFDI addenda for Mexican electronic
invoicing on Odoo Enterprise and adds invoice / partner fields used by
the addenda XML.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

1. Go to **Accounting > Customers > Customers**.
2. Open the Audi partner.
3. Add **Addenda Audi** to the partner addendas.
4. Fill supplier email and supplier number when visible.

Usage
=====

1. Create an invoice for a partner with **Addenda Audi**.
2. Fill the Audi fields on the **Audi Addenda** tab and line product
   references.
3. Confirm and stamp the invoice.
